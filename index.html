Methodology & Data Notes

Data Source: This distribution is built directly from a full UTXO set snapshot of the Bitcoin blockchain taken at Block #935,000 (mined on February 4, 2026). All unspent transaction outputs (UTXOs) sharing the same locking script (scriptPubKey) have been aggregated to compute each unique address's total balance.

Why balances below 1,000 satoshis (0.00001 BTC) are excluded

  • Network Dust & Spam: Outputs at standard dust limits (such as 330 or 546 satoshis) are uneconomical to spend because transaction fees often exceed their value.
  • Inscriptions & Metadata Protocols: Protocols such as Ordinals, BRC-20, Runes, and cryptographic timestamping services generate millions of minimal-value UTXOs (330-600 sats) to anchor data rather than store financial value.
  • Unspendable Outputs: Zero-value outputs (such as OP_RETURN data carriers) do not represent spendable bitcoins.

Important Limitations to Keep in Mind

  • One Person, Multiple Addresses: Modern Bitcoin wallets generate a new address for every incoming transaction to protect user privacy. Conversely, a single individual or entity often splits their holdings across dozens of addresses. Therefore, an address ranking is not an exact ranking of individual people.
  • Centralized Exchanges (CEX) Excluded at User Level: Off-chain user accounts held on centralized exchanges (such as Binance, Kraken, or Coinbase) are not visible on the Bitcoin blockchain. Exchange reserves are pooled into a handful of massive institutional cold wallets (which appear at the far right of the chart), while millions of retail exchange users are not represented individually in this on-chain dataset.
